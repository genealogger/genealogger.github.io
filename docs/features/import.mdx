# Smart Ingest & Import Engine

Importing shouldn't be a chore—or a privacy risk. Our smart ingest engine turns raw scans, photos, and documents into structured records automatically while keeping **100% of your data private and on your device**.

---

### Built Private By Design
We won't upload your sensitive photos and documents to third-party cloud AI models. Our app handles all intelligence **locally on your desktop**. 

* **On-Device OCR & Entity Linking:** Document text is read and matched locally.
* **On-Device Face Detection:** Facial scanning and captioning never leave your machine.
* **Offline-Friendly:** The app operates fully offline. The only lightweight API requests ever made are for map tile rendering and location autocomplete suggestions.

---

## The Import Workflow

### 1. Drag & Drop
Simply drop your files anywhere into the workspace. The app automatically places incoming files into the **Needs Attention** tab in your sidebar.

### 2. Automated Local Processing
As files land, background processing immediately gets to work on your machine:

* **Duplicate Prevention:** Skips any files previously added to your workspace.
* **Multi-Page Auto-Grouping:** Identifies file sequences (e.g., `- 1`, `(1)`, `1 of 2`) and stacks them into single records. *(Manual unmerging available).*
* **Local Document OCR:** Extracts full text from documents and scans for existing people and places in your network.
* **Visual Recognition:** Identifies faces in photos and generates descriptive initial captions locally.
* **Metadata Extraction:** Reads embedded EXIF keywords and captions. Direct matches automatically link to your existing catalog, while new keywords are queued for easy 1-click conversion.

### 3. Quick Review & Processing
Draft records are immediately usable across your workspace—no need to stop and edit right away.

* **Batch Editing:** Select multiple drafts to assign common sources, dates, or tags simultaneously.
* **Smart Badges & Flags:** Visual badges highlight draft status, while flags alert you if essential fields (like source or date) are missing.
* **Process Record:** Once you're satisfied with the metadata, click **Process** to clear it from your inbox.
