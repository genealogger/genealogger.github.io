# Topics: Evidence-Based Storytelling

Facts and records only tell half the story—**Topics** bring them together. A Topic is a rich narrative note that connects multiple records, people, and places across your archive into a single, cohesive timeline, complete with auto-generated citations.

---

### 🛡️ Local, Bi-Directional & Connected
Just like imports, your Topics, notes, and evidence maps are processed and stored **100% locally on your computer**.

* **Bi-Directional Linking:** Linked records automatically display backlinks. View any record to instantly see every Topic where it is cited.
* **Structured Organization:** Organize your research using a flexible nested folder tree (`Folders > Sub-folders > Topics`).

---

## How to Create & Enrich a Topic

### 1. Create a Topic Note
1. Navigate to the **Notes** tab in the sidebar.
2. Create or select a folder in your workspace tree (e.g., `Smith Family > Immigration`).
3. Click **New Note** and give your Topic a title (e.g., `John Smith's Passage to New York`).

---

### 2. Connect Supporting Records
You can link primary evidence and supporting documents to your Topic in four flexible ways:

* ➕ **Manual Selection:** Click the **+** button next to *Linked Records* to pick records manually.
* 🔍 **Query Search:** Click the **Search +** icon to locate records using name queries or advanced search syntax, then click **Save to Topic**.
* 📁 **Direct Desktop Drag & Drop:** Drag files straight onto your note canvas.
  * *New files* automatically become new Draft Records in your workspace.
  * *Existing files* are recognized by hash and linked immediately—no duplicates created.
* 📌 **From the Record View:** Link any record to a Topic directly while inspecting it on its individual record page.

---

### 3. Write & Annotate with Smart Context
As you draft your narrative, the app actively assists your research:

* **Inline Smart Auto-Suggest:** Start typing (e.g., *"This is the immigration story of John Smith..."*) and the app auto-suggests relevant records, people, and places. Click **Link** to attach them instantly.
* **Interactive Side-by-Side Reference:** Click any linked record in your sidebar to view its full text, transcription, and image attachments without leaving your writing flow.
* **Embedded Visual Cards:** Drag a linked record directly into your prose to insert a visual card featuring browseable images, captions, and title metadata.
* **Auto-Generated GEDCOM Citations:** As you cite and drag records into your narrative, your reference section automatically updates in order of appearance using standard GEDCOM-style formatting.

---

### 4. Export Your Research
Once your story is complete, click **Export** to take your work anywhere:

| Format | Output Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **PDF** | Formatted print/share-ready document with embedded media and GEDCOM references. |
| **Markdown** | Raw, clean text file with full Markdown syntax for porting to other tools. |
| **Plain Text / Files** | Stripped document export with attached raw source files for archiving. |
