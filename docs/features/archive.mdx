# Archival Workflows: Boxes & Photo Albums

Real research starts in the real world. Whether you inherited a box of mixed family letters or a fragile, 1920s magnetic photo album, our app provides dedicated workflows designed to preserve physical context while digitizing your collection.

---

## 📦 The Box Workflow: Systematic Ingestion

Cataloging a physical box of mixed letters, clippings, and loose photographs can feel overwhelming. The **Box Workflow** gives you a systematic, friction-free way to turn raw physical containers into structured digital archives.

### 1. Create a Source & Auto-ID
Use the **Source Wizard** to create a entry for your physical box (e.g., *"Box #3 - Great Grandma's Attic Items"*). On save, the app creates a dedicated Source page where newly added items are **automatically assigned unique record IDs**.

### 2. Flexible File Attachment & Ingest
* **Quick-Edit & Drag-and-Drop:** Drag scanned files directly from your desktop onto a record slot to attach them immediately.
* **Sidebar Queue:** Queue an entire folder of scans in your sidebar. As you drag files onto specific record entries, they automatically clear from your queue.

### 3. Smart Source Statistics
The **Stats Tab** acts as your archival compass, showing you where to focus your effort:
* 🔗 **Most Connected Records:** Highlights items linked to the highest number of people, places, and events.
* 🔍 **Orphaned Records:** Identifies unlinked items that need research or tagged relationships.
* 📊 **Top Entities:** Automatically counts and ranks the most frequently cited people, places, and keywords across the box.

---

## 🖼️ The Photo Album Workflow: Context & Layout Preservation

In antique photo albums, **the layout tells as much of a story as the pictures themselves**. The Photo Album workflow lets you digitize individual photos while preserving full-page spatial context and front/back details.

### 1. Page-by-Page Digital Reconstruction
* **Page Overview Scans:** Upload a full-page scan (e.g., `Page 1 - Overview`) so you never lose how photos were originally arranged side-by-side.
* **Individual Photo Placement:** Drag cropped photos onto the digital page board and reorder them easily.

### 2. Multi-Scan Record Merging
Photographs often have critical details on their reverse sides. Easily merge multiple scans into a single record to combine:
* **Front + Back Scans:** Keep handwritten dates and notes on the back attached directly to the front image.
* **High-Res Upgrades:** Merge higher-resolution rescans without losing existing tags or metadata.

### 3. Review & Export
* 🎬 **Built-In Slideshow:** Click the **Slideshow** button to present or review the entire album chronologically page-by-page.
* 💾 **Structured Export:** Export the album as a single, multi-page PDF or an organized image folder automatically named `Album Name - Page X Image Y`.
